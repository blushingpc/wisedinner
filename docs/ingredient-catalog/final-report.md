## Scaling and limits

Baseline: 21 cases, including six profiles at seeds 0/7/19, all 18 ordinary cases feasible/deterministic; median 529 ms. Progressive catalogs 149/500/1,000/4,787 entries: all **72** ordinary cases feasible/deterministic with hard constraints re-evaluated, median selection-plus-solve times **660/692/654/745 ms**. Total scaling cases: 85. Runtime searches at most **96** recipes. Difficult cases remain honestly infeasible and can take 4 to 4.8 seconds. Sampled desktop RSS reached 375 MB, including generator/benchmark data, not isolated solver peak. Final staging generation 33 ms and validation/approval/compilation/report work about 184 ms.

Larger catalogs are quarantined computational stress, not source approvals. Benchmarks use 83 legacy anchors; the default no-anchor policy does not have equivalent feasibility evidence. Substitution coverage is non-monotonic (37/22/25/41 of 54 sampled slots across sizes). No cheaper-week, global-optimum or iPhone claim. The approved-only ten-SKU subset is infeasible under the unchanged typical twelve-item floor, protein target and calorie-share rules. It cannot replace published coverage. Details: [benchmark report](benchmark-report.md) and raw JSONs.

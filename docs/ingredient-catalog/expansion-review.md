# Initial batch review

October 4, local only. The fixed manifest approves 66 internal ingredient-matched preparations, not 4,704 customer-ready meals. Digest pins the entire reviewed generator revision; a changed revision requires fresh approval. Every proposed ID was inspected for its ingredient family, edible/raw quantities, declared technique, cooking time and generated directions. Representative egg, beef/brown-rice, chicken/lentil and lentil/edamame instructions were read in full. Tests check every candidate against the same ingredient-specific rendering and reject alterations, missing steps, role mismatches and portion contradictions.

Scope: six reviewed single proteins plus five complementary dual-protein combinations, three dry grains, plain frozen broccoli, two real preparations. Absorbed bowls retain juices and reduce excess water; brothy soups retain cooking liquid and add water. This changes technique/texture, not just titles. Different meal slots, names and portion tweaks do not count as distinct identity. No unlisted seasoning, stock, oil or sauce contributes nutrients. The meals are intentionally plain and still need kitchen/taste review before customer curation. No new customer-visible count, photos or product promises.

Quantities: grains are 80 g dry, single raw meat 180 g, lentils 90 g dry, edamame 180 g shelled frozen, eggs three large. Dual proteins use 120 g each except lentils 50 g dry and eggs two large. Broccoli is 140 g frozen edible. These are authored portions, not automatic pack finishing. Whole purchased packs remain priced; leftovers stay explicit. Every approved SKU is one of the ten generic-reviewed references and has reviewed inherent metadata. Package cross-contact is still unverified.

The existing authored compiler groups candidates into base recipes and explicit protein_swap variants with complete quantity/direction overrides. Photo metadata remains null, the neutral fallback. Candidate generation precedes a separately saved approval manifest; all unresolved entries remain in quarantine with stable IDs and reasons. No published coverage is dropped. The standalone ten-SKU staging artifact cannot meet the existing twelve-item floor for typical profiles and must not replace the production snapshot.

Cooking endpoint guidance was checked against [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures), accessed October 4 local: poultry 165 F, ground beef and egg dishes 160 F. Time estimates are not kitchen timing proof; instructions require tenderness and temperature rather than treating the timer as safety evidence.
